Questions

The things people
ask us first.

As a working rule, a 1 kW rooftop array needs about 8–10 square metres of shade-free area. A 3 kW home system therefore needs roughly 25–30 square metres. The exact figure depends on module wattage and how the panels are laid out around water tanks, staircases and parapets — which is why we survey before quoting.

Yes. We guide you through the PM Surya Ghar application, the DISCOM net-metering process and the inspection that follows commissioning. Subsidy amounts and eligibility are decided by the scheme and your DISCOM, so we confirm the current figure for your case rather than promise a fixed number.

We work with multiple brands — including Adani, Tata Power Solar, Waaree and INA Solar for modules, with GoodWe inverters and branded wires, cables and accessories. We match the configuration to your budget rather than pushing a single brand.

Physical installation for a typical home rooftop is usually a matter of days once the structure and material are on site. The longer part of the timeline is usually approvals and net metering, which sit with the DISCOM. We give you a realistic schedule during the survey instead of an optimistic one.

Mostly cleaning. Dust is the biggest enemy of generation in Rajasthan, so panels need periodic washing with water — no detergents or abrasives. Beyond that, an annual check of connections, earthing and inverter logs is enough. We offer servicing for systems we install.
